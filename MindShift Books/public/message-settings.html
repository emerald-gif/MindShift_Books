Message settings

Choose what other people can see about you in Messages. Changes save automatically.

Read receipts
Let people know when you’ve seen their messages. If you turn this off, you won’t see when others have seen yours either.
Activity status
Show when you’re online (“Active now”) and when you were last active (for example “Active 2h ago”). If you turn this off, nobody can see either one for you, and you won’t see them for anyone else.
Active nowActive 2h ago
Typing indicator
Let people see “typing…” while you write to them. If you turn this off, you won’t see when others are typing either.
Recording indicator
Let people see “recording audio…” while you record a voice message. If you turn this off, you won’t see it for others either.

These settings apply to your direct messages. Messages from people you follow arrive in your inbox, and messages from people you don’t follow arrive as message requests.
