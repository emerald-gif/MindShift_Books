Part 1 Terms of Use

About MindShift Books

MindShift Books began as a digital publishing platform selling eBooks in PDF format, and has grown into a content and community platform. Today it includes our eBook store; a shared feed of articles, posts, polls and reposts; member profiles; private messages; creator tools such as Profile Insights; and a free affiliate program. Our website is mindshiftbooks.shop and you can reach us at contact@mindshiftbooks.shop.

By using MindShift Books you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

Who can use MindShift Books

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. If you are under 18, please use MindShift Books with the permission and guidance of a parent or guardian. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we will remove it.

Browsing articles and the bookstore does not need an account. Writing, following, messaging, saving, joining the affiliate program and buying books do.

Your account

You can sign up with an email and password, or continue with Google or Facebook. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details safe and for everything that happens under your account.

  • One account per person. Keep your profile information accurate, and do not pretend to be someone else.
  • Tell us straight away at contact@mindshiftbooks.shop if you think someone has accessed your account.
  • You can stop using MindShift Books at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account (see Your rights).
  • We may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms, puts others at risk, or is used for fraud or abuse.

Community rules

MindShift Books is a place for ideas, learning and encouragement. To keep it that way, please do not post, send or share:

  • Anything illegal, or that encourages or helps someone commit a crime.
  • Hateful, abusive or harassing content, threats, or bullying, including in private messages.
  • Sexually explicit content, or anything that sexualises or endangers children.
  • Scams, misleading claims, fake offers, or attempts to get money or personal details from people.
  • Spam, fake engagement, mass unsolicited messages, or repeated promotion that is not wanted.
  • Other people's private information (doxxing), or content that impersonates another person or brand.
  • Malware, harmful links, or attempts to break, scrape or overload the platform.
  • Work you do not have the right to share, including copyrighted text, images, audio or video.

Polls, comments and reposts follow the same rules. Content that breaks them may be removed, and accounts may be warned, restricted or suspended, depending on how serious or repeated the problem is.

Your content and what you let us do with it

You keep ownership of everything you publish: articles, posts, polls, comments, photos, videos, voice notes and anything else. You are responsible for it, and it should be your own work or something you are allowed to share.

By publishing, you give MindShift Books a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, store, display, distribute and format that content on the platform, so other members can read and engage with it. This includes showing previews of it (for example a link card or a shareable image) and sharing it on our own channels, such as our official account, our website and our social media pages, with your name credited. This only applies to content you have made public. You can ask us to stop featuring something at any time.

When you delete a piece of content or your account, we stop showing it. Copies held in short-term backups, or already shared by other people (for example a repost or a message), may remain for a while or on their side. Views expressed in member content belong to their authors, not to MindShift Books.

Private messages

Members can message each other, including with photos, videos and voice notes. Messages are between you and the people you send them to.

  • How they are stored. Messages are stored on our servers so we can deliver them and show them on all your devices. They are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Who can read them. We do not read private messages as a matter of routine. We may look at specific messages when needed to investigate a report, prevent serious harm, enforce these terms, or respond to a valid legal request.
  • Your controls. In Message settings you can choose read receipts, activity status and typing indicators. You can also block people. Messages from people you don't follow arrive as message requests until you accept them.
  • Be careful what you send. Anyone who receives a message or file can keep or share it. Do not share passwords, bank details or other sensitive information in chats.

Reporting, moderation and takedowns

If you see content or behaviour that breaks these rules, email contact@mindshiftbooks.shop with a link to the content or the username involved and a short description. We review reports as quickly as we can and may remove content, warn, restrict or suspend accounts. If you think we got something wrong, reply to our message and we will take another look.

Copyright complaints. If you believe something on MindShift Books copies your work without permission, email us with: a description of your work, the link to where it appears, your contact details, and a statement that you are the owner or authorised to act for them. We will review it and may remove the content. The person who posted it can respond if they believe the removal was a mistake.

Buying books

What you get. All books sold on MindShift Books are digital eBooks in PDF format. When payment is confirmed you receive a confirmation email with your download link(s), and you can also find them any time under Order History in Settings. Download links do not expire. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or promotions folder.

Payments. Payments are processed securely by Paystack. MindShift Books never sees or stores your card details. By paying, you also agree to Paystack's terms.

Prices and currency. Prices are set in Nigerian Naira (₦). If you browse from outside Nigeria we may show an approximate dollar amount for convenience, based on a daily exchange rate. The amount you actually pay is the Naira price. Paying in dollars is not available yet.

Vouchers and promotions. Vouchers, discounts and promo credits (such as a welcome or profile voucher) have no cash value, cannot be transferred or exchanged for money, and may have conditions, such as which orders they apply to or an expiry date. These are shown when the voucher is issued or applied. We may change or end promotions at any time, but not for orders already paid.

Refunds. Because our products are digital and delivered instantly, all sales are generally final. We review refund requests case by case in these situations:

  • You were charged but did not receive your download link and could not access it under Order History.
  • You were charged twice for the same order.
  • You received a file that was corrupted or unreadable.

To ask for a refund, email contact@mindshiftbooks.shop with your order reference and a short description of the problem. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Your licence to the book. When you buy a book you get a personal, non-transferable licence to read it for your own use. You may not share, resell, copy or distribute it, or claim it as your own, without our written permission.

Part 2 Privacy Policy

Our approach to privacy

MindShift Books is responsible for the personal information described here. We follow the principles of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: we collect only what we need, tell you what we do with it, keep it secure, and let you control it. Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@mindshiftbooks.shop.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing, and we do not run advertising. No ad networks have access to your data.

What we collect and why

  • Account and profile. Name, email, username, password (handled by our sign-in provider), profile photo, cover, bio, location, job title and social links you choose to add. If you sign in with Google or Facebook we receive the basic details they share, such as your name, email and picture. A phone number or contact email is optional, and it only appears on your public profile if you turn that on.
  • What you create. Articles, posts, polls, comments, reposts, drafts, and the photos, videos and voice notes you upload.
  • Messages. The messages and files you send, who you send them to, when, and whether they were read, plus your message settings and blocked list.
  • Activity. Likes, follows, saves, shares, reading and viewing activity, the topics you pick, and notifications you receive. We use this to run the feed, show you relevant content, and give creators their insights.
  • Orders and payments. Your name, email, order reference, the books you bought and any voucher used. Paystack processes the payment. We only get confirmation that it went through and the email used. We never see your card number.
  • Affiliate details. Your referral clicks, sign-ups and sales, and the bank account details needed to pay your commission.
  • Device and technical data. Your IP address, browser and device type, and error information, so the site works and stays secure. We look up your approximate country from your connection to choose which currency to display. If you are not signed in, we store a random visitor ID on your device so we can count profile and article views once instead of repeatedly.
  • Support. Emails and details you send us when you ask for help.

How we use it

  • To run your account, the feed, messaging, notifications and your profile.
  • To deliver your purchases, handle refunds and pay affiliate commissions.
  • To send emails you need: order confirmations, account and affiliate updates, security notices, and the activity digest if you turn it on. We do not add you to a marketing list without your consent.
  • To keep the community safe, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our rules.
  • To understand how the platform is used so we can fix problems and improve it.
  • To meet our legal duties.

Our reasons for using your information are: to provide the service you asked for (contract), your consent where we ask for it, our legitimate interests in running and securing the platform, and legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Other members can see what you choose to make public: your profile, posts, articles, comments, polls, and your follower and following lists. Private messages go only to the people you send them to.

We use these companies to run MindShift Books. They handle your data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, our database and security rules.
  • Cloudinary: storing and delivering photos, videos and voice notes.
  • Brevo: sending our emails.
  • Paystack: processing payments.
  • Render: hosting our servers.
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare / jsDelivr (content delivery): fonts and code libraries your browser loads, which means your IP address reaches them.
  • Exchange-rate and country-lookup services: to show approximate dollar prices to visitors outside Nigeria.

We may also share information when the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect people's safety or our rights, or if MindShift Books is ever merged with or acquired by another business (we would tell you first).

Where your data is processed

Our providers run their systems in different countries, including outside Nigeria. When your information is processed abroad, we rely on our providers' security commitments and contracts, and we only share what is needed to provide the service.

Cookies and storage on your device

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. To work properly, the site stores small pieces of information on your device, such as:

  • your sign-in session,
  • your theme choice (light, dark or automatic) and your currency choice,
  • a copy of your profile name and photo so pages load faster,
  • drafts of things you are writing, and your feed topics,
  • a random visitor ID if you are not signed in (used to count views).

You can clear this in your browser settings. If you do, you will be signed out and some preferences or drafts will be lost.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account we remove your account and order records within 7 business days. We may keep limited information longer where we must (for example payment and tax records, or to prevent fraud and enforce our rules), and deleted data can remain in backups for a short period before it is permanently erased.

Keeping it secure

We use secure connections, access controls and database security rules to protect your information, and we only give our team the access they need. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Your rights and choices

Under Nigerian data protection law you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong or out of date (you can also edit most of it yourself in your profile and settings);
  • delete your information and account;
  • limit or stop certain uses, or object to them;
  • give you a copy of your data in a usable format;
  • stop sending you optional emails, or take back a consent you gave.

To use any of these, email contact@mindshiftbooks.shop from the address on your account. We may need to confirm it is really you, and we aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not happy with how we handled your information, you also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

MindShift Books is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. Members under 18 should use the platform with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child's information was shared with us, email us and we will delete it.

Contact us

Questions about these terms, your privacy, a report, or a copyright complaint? Email contact@mindshiftbooks.shop. We typically reply within a day, and within 2 business days for refund requests. You can also visit our Help Center.
