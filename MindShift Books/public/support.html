Getting Started

MindShift Books is a social reading and publishing ecosystem where people discover ideas, connect with creators, read books, and — eventually — publish and sell their own. It brings together three things: Read — articles, posts, free eBooks, and books on mindset, personal growth, money, productivity, relationships, wellness, career, and meaningful living; Connect — build a profile, follow authors, write your own articles and posts, like, comment, save, and grow an audience; and Publish (coming later) — creators will be able to publish their own eBooks, build an audience around their work, and sell directly to readers. You don't have to arrive with a book — you can start by simply reading, and see where it takes you.
No — reading articles and browsing the bookstore is open to everyone. You'll need a free account to write articles or posts, follow authors, save articles, build a profile, join the affiliate program, or buy a book.
Tap the menu and choose "Sign Up" or "Log In," or head straight to /signup. You can sign up with an email and password, or continue with Google or Facebook — whichever's faster.
Yes, completely free. Creating an account, reading, writing, following authors, and joining the affiliate program never cost anything — the only thing you ever pay for is a book you choose to buy.

Articles & Community

A Post is a quick text update with up to 5 photos — good for a thought or an update. An Article is a full write-up with its own cover image and category, meant for something more in-depth. Both show up on your profile and in your followers' feeds.
Tap "Create" in the menu (or on your profile) and choose Post or Article. You'll need to be signed in first — if you aren't, it'll prompt you to log in or create a free account.
Yes — visit an author's profile and tap Follow, and their new articles and posts will start showing up in your feed. The more you read and follow, the more your feed on the homepage gets tailored to what you're into.
Yes — tap the save icon on any article, then find everything you've saved under "Saved Articles" in the menu.
In the post composer tap Add poll, write your question and add 2 to 4 options. People vote once, and the results update as votes come in. When a poll ends it shows final results. Polls can appear in your followers' feeds and in reposts, like any other post.
Open the post and tap the ⋯ menu. You'll see Manage post with Edit, Turn off commenting, Hide like count, Pin to profile, Copy link, Share as image and Delete. Changes to a post usually show for everyone within a few seconds.
A repost puts someone else's post or article on your own profile and in your followers' feeds, with their name still on it. Tap the repost icon under any post. To send something privately, tap the share icon and choose a chat to send it straight to someone in Messages, or copy the link.
Yes. Type @ and their name in a post, article or comment to mention them. They'll get a notification. If you paste a link into a post, MindShift Books shows a preview card with the page's title, image and site, so people can see what it is before they tap.

Profile & Insights

Go to My Profile from the menu and tap edit to update your photo, cover image, and bio. This is also where your articles, posts, and follower count live.
Open "My Insights" in the menu (or Profile Insights in Settings) to see how your profile is doing over time — views, follower growth, and how your articles are performing.
In Settings, tap "Share Profile" to copy your profile link — it points straight to your public page at /profile/@yourusername.

Sign in and open Order History in Settings — everything tied to your account shows up there, no need to search by email.
Yes — every book in that order gets its own download link, both in your confirmation email and under Order History in Settings.

Affiliate Program

It's a free way to earn money by recommending MindShift Books. You get a personal referral link to the whole catalog — share it anywhere (Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, a blog) — and you earn a commission on every book bought through it.
Tap "Become an Affiliate" in the menu or footer, or go straight to /affiliate and hit "Become an affiliate." Sign in (or create a free account) and your unique referral link is generated instantly — no application review, no minimum followers.
15% of the actual price paid on every book sold through your link — including if it sold at a discount. It's tracked and credited automatically, no codes to enter at checkout.
There's no deadline. Once someone clicks your link, your credit for them is remembered until they sign up — even weeks later — and it carries through even if they sign up with Google or Facebook instead of clicking straight through.
Payouts run automatically every Monday, straight to the bank account on your affiliate profile, once your available balance reaches the ₦5,000 minimum. Below that, your balance just keeps growing until it clears the threshold — no need to request anything.
Sign in and open your affiliate dashboard — it shows your link, clicks, sign-ups, books sold, and running balance in real time. Your bank details and payout history live under Payout.
Yes, from your payout page — unless you already have a payout queued for the coming Monday, in which case those details are locked until it's paid out, then editable again right away.
It's completely free, forever, and you don't need to have bought a book yourself to start recommending them.

Messages & Safety

Open Messages from the menu (or tap Message on someone's profile), then start a new chat and pick the person. You can send text, photos, videos and voice notes.
If someone you don't follow messages you, it goes to Message requests instead of your inbox, so you decide whether to chat. You can accept it, ignore it, or block the person. Messages from people you follow arrive in your inbox as normal.
Yes. In Messages tap the ⋮ menu, then Message settings. You can switch off read receipts, activity status ("Active now") and typing indicators. Note that if you turn one off, you also stop seeing it from other people.
Open the chat with them, tap the menu and choose Block user. They can no longer message you. You can see and undo your blocks from Messages ⋮ → Blocked users.
Email contact@mindshiftbooks.shop with a link to the post or the username, and a line on what's wrong. We review reports as fast as we can and may remove content or restrict accounts. Copyright complaints are handled the same way; see the Terms for what to include.
Messages are only shared with the people you send them to, and we don't read them as a routine. They are stored on our servers so they can reach you on every device, and they are not end-to-end encrypted. We may review specific messages if needed to investigate a report, prevent serious harm or respond to a valid legal request. Details are in the Terms.
