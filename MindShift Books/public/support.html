Getting Started

MindShift Books is a social reading and publishing ecosystem where people discover ideas, connect with creators, read books, and — eventually — publish and sell their own. It brings together three things: Read — articles, posts, free eBooks, and books on mindset, personal growth, money, productivity, relationships, wellness, career, and meaningful living; Connect — build a profile, follow authors, write your own articles and posts, like, comment, save, and grow an audience; and Publish (coming later) — creators will be able to publish their own eBooks, build an audience around their work, and sell directly to readers. You don't have to arrive with a book — you can start by simply reading, and see where it takes you.

Yes, completely free. Creating an account, reading, writing, following authors, and joining the affiliate program never cost anything — the only thing you ever pay for is a book you choose to buy.

Articles & Community

A Post is a quick text update with up to 5 photos — good for a thought or an update. An Article is a full write-up with its own cover image and category, meant for something more in-depth. Both show up on your profile and in your followers' feeds.
Tap "Create" in the menu (or on your profile) and choose Post or Article. You'll need to be signed in first — if you aren't, it'll prompt you to log in or create a free account.

Profile & Insights

Go to My Profile from the menu and tap edit to update your photo, cover image, and bio. This is also where your articles, posts, and follower count live.
Open "My Insights" in the menu (or Profile Insights in Settings) to see how your profile is doing over time — views, follower growth, and how your articles are performing.
In Settings, tap "Share Profile" to copy your profile link — it points straight to your public page at /profile/@yourusername.

Affiliate Program

It's a free way to earn money by recommending MindShift Books. You get a personal referral link to the whole catalog — share it anywhere (Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, a blog) — and you earn a commission on every book bought through it.
Tap "Become an Affiliate" in the menu or footer, or go straight to /affiliate and hit "Become an affiliate." Sign in (or create a free account) and your unique referral link is generated instantly — no application review, no minimum followers.
15% of the actual price paid on every book sold through your link — including if it sold at a discount. It's tracked and credited automatically, no codes to enter at checkout.
There's no deadline. Once someone clicks your link, your credit for them is remembered until they sign up — even weeks later — and it carries through even if they sign up with Google or Facebook instead of clicking straight through.
Payouts run automatically every Monday, straight to the bank account on your affiliate profile, once your available balance reaches the ₦5,000 minimum. Below that, your balance just keeps growing until it clears the threshold — no need to request anything.
Sign in and open your affiliate dashboard — it shows your link, clicks, sign-ups, books sold, and running balance in real time. Your bank details and payout history live under Payout.
Yes, from your payout page — unless you already have a payout queued for the coming Monday, in which case those details are locked until it's paid out, then editable again right away.
It's completely free, forever, and you don't need to have bought a book yourself to start recommending them.
