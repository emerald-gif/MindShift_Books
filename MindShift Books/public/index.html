Questions? Answered.

Is MindShift Books free to join?

Yes. Creating an account is free and needs no card. You only pay if you choose to buy a book from the bookstore.

Can I download the free eBooks?

Yes. Open any free eBook to read it online or download it to keep, and save your favourites to your wishlist.

How does the affiliate program work?

You get your own link to share. You earn 15% commission on every book sale made through it. It's free to join and you don't need to buy anything.

Can I publish my own writing?

Yes. Create an account, then head to Write to publish posts and articles and build your profile.

Can I buy a book without an account?

Yes. Buy as a guest with just a name and an email and the book arrives by email. If you sign up later with the same email, your books are waiting in your library.

Is there anything for students?

Student Resources has study guides, educational books and eBooks, tips for academic success and tools to boost your learning.

What currency are books priced in?

Books in the bookstore are priced in naira (₦).
