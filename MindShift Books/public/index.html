Frequently Asked Questions

What is MindShift Books?

MindShift Books is an online eBook store. You browse titles on psychology, money, and personal growth, pay securely by card, and receive the PDF instantly by email — no shipping, no waiting.

What can I do on this site?

Browse and search our catalog, read a free preview of some titles, add books to a cart and check out, save books to a wishlist for later, and view your past orders and receipts under My Account.

Do I need an account to buy a book?

Yes. A free account lets us send your eBook to the right email address and keep a record of what you've bought so you can re-download it later from My Account without searching your inbox.

Why does MindShift Books offer "Sign in with Google"?

It's a faster alternative to creating an email/password account. We only use it to confirm your identity and get your name and email address to create your account and send your order — MindShift Books never posts on your behalf or accesses anything else in your Google account.

How do I receive my eBook after paying?

Payments are processed securely by Paystack. As soon as payment is confirmed, the PDF is emailed to the address on your account automatically, and it also stays available under My Account.

What is the wishlist for?

Tap the heart icon on any book to save it to your wishlist so you can find and buy it later without searching for it again. Your wishlist is tied to your account and available on any device you sign into.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Checkout is handled entirely by Paystack — MindShift Books never sees or stores your card details.

Where can I read the privacy policy and terms?

See our Terms & Privacy page for full details on how your data is used and stored.

How do I contact support?

Visit our Support page or email contact@mindshiftbooks.shop and we'll get back to you.
