Link previews in posts

Paste a link into a post and it turns into a clean preview card with the title, image and site, right in the composer and on the post.

How to use it
  1. Start a new post.
  2. Paste any link.
  3. The preview card appears. Publish as usual.

Tap any image to view it

Tap a photo in a post and it opens full screen, with the like, comment and share buttons right there so you never have to go back.

Share posts straight to a chat

Found something a friend should see? Send a post or article to someone directly in Messages.

How to use it
  1. Tap the share icon on a post or article.
  2. Choose a chat.
  3. It lands in their conversation.

Messages

Chat with anyone on MindShift Books. Message writers, creators and other readers directly.

Voice notes, photos and videos
Send them right inside the chat. Pick several photos and they arrive together as one album.
Reactions
Press and hold a message to react with an emoji.
Replies
Reply to a specific message so everyone knows what you’re answering.
How to start
  1. Tap the Messages icon, or
  2. Open any profile and tap the Message button.
