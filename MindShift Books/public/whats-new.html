More free eBooks, in a new Top Free list

We’ve added more free books, almost 100 now, from timeless classics to mindset and money reads. On the Books page, Free eBooks is a ranked Top Free list: a small cover, the book’s number, its title and author, three books at a time. Swipe sideways for more.

A banner that keeps moving
On the Free eBooks page, the banner at the top now rotates on its own through a fresh pick of books. Touch it to pause, swipe it yourself, or tap a dot to jump.
Shelves that mix it up
Trending Now and every category shelf are shuffled each time you visit, so you don’t see the same books in the same order.

Download free eBooks and save them for later

Found a book you love? Keep it. Every free eBook now has a Download button and a heart to save it to your wishlist. In My Library, filters help you find things fast.

How to use it
  1. Tap any free eBook to open its details.
  2. Tap Download. It says “Preparing…” for a moment, then the book is saved to your phone as an EPUB you can open in any reading app.
  3. You can also tap Download at the top of the reading screen while you read.
  4. Tap the heart on any free eBook, in the Top Free list, on the Free eBooks page, or inside its details, to save it. Find it any time in My Library, under Wishlist, with a Read free button.
  5. In My Library, use the filter chips at the top of Saved (All, Articles, Posts) and Wishlist (All, Books, Free eBooks) to see just what you’re looking for.

Every download opens with a MindShift Books title page and ends with a page of more free reads.

Comment without leaving the video

Tap the comment bubble on a video and the comments slide up from the bottom. The video shrinks to the top so you can still see it, and everything works just like comments on a post: read, like, reply and write your own.

How to use it
  1. Open any video from your feed.
  2. Tap the comment bubble.
  3. To close, swipe the handle down, tap the video, or tap the ✕. The video grows back to full screen.

When you tap the comment box, the comments lift above your keyboard so you can see what you’re typing.

Easier replies and nicer comments

Replying to a comment is now smoother on posts and articles.

Reply right in the comment box
Tap Reply and the box at the bottom turns into “Replying to” that person, with their @name ready. Tap ✕ to cancel.
Quick reactions
On posts, tap a heart, fire, clap or another emoji above the comment box to add it in one tap.
See your comment land
When you post, the page scrolls to your comment and highlights it for a moment.
Friendlier loading
Comments show a gentle placeholder while they load, and an empty post invites you to start the conversation. If replies can’t load, a Try again button appears.

A fresher bookstore

New Releases and Recommended for You now swipe sideways, with big covers and the next book peeking in. Tap the heart on a cover to save it to your wishlist.

Cleaner book page
A big cover, the price and your savings in one card, and Add to Cart and Preview right under it. As you scroll, an Add to Cart bar waits at the bottom of your phone.
Books for you in your feed
The books shelf in your feed now looks like a post from MindShift Books. Tap a cover to open the book, or tap ⋯ then Hide for today.

Affiliate dashboard, upgraded

If you’re an affiliate, your dashboard is clearer and faster.

Your balance up top
See your available balance, total earned and what’s been paid out, with a note on how close you are to the minimum payout.
Readers you’ve brought in
It has its own page now. Find Readers in the menu.
Safer bank changes
Updating your bank details is a full-screen, step-by-step flow: confirm the 6-digit code we email you, enter your new details, done.
Honest numbers and no more spinners
Link clicks count real visitors once a day, sales count books, and pages open instantly instead of waiting on a spinner.

Smoother everywhere

No more sign-up banner flash
Signed-in readers no longer see the “Continue Reading Free” box for a split second when opening an article.
Counts that show up
Likes, comments and reposts on profile pages now appear reliably, including comments on articles.
Videos in your feed
Feed videos always sit in a neat portrait frame.

Student Resources

A new space for learners. Student Resources has four sections: study guides and learning materials, educational books and eBooks, tips for academic success, and tools to boost your learning. It’s just opening, so more is being added over time.

How to find it
  1. Tap the blue Student Resources bar at the top of the store or your feed.
  2. Pick a section.
  3. Check back often, new things will show up there.

On your feed you can tap the ✕ on the bar to hide it for the rest of the day.

Cleaner People to follow

The People to follow cards in your feed are tidier. Photos are smaller so you see more people at once, each card shows how many followers they have, and the Follow button is cleaner. Swipe sideways to browse, tap ✕ to remove someone, or tap See all for the full list.

Link previews in chats

Paste a link in a message and a preview card shows above the text box before you send it. In the chat, the link opens as a clean card with the title, image and site.

How to use it
  1. Paste a link in a message.
  2. Check the preview above the text box. Tap the ✕ if you’d rather send it without one.
  3. Send. Links people send you show a card too.

Chat info, search and shared media

Tap a person’s name or photo at the top of a chat to open everything about that conversation.

Shared media and links
Every photo, video and link you’ve exchanged, in one place. Press and hold a photo to select and delete.
Search in this chat
Find a message fast, and pull in older messages when you need them.
See who reacted
Tap the reaction under a message to see who reacted with what. Tap your own to take it back.

Cleaner, smoother chats

Messages from the same person now sit together with one time stamp, and “Seen” shows only under your latest message. The chat list is cleaner too, and typing on iPhone no longer leaves gaps or jumps around.

Books for you, right in your feed

Swipe a shelf of real books as you scroll, with the cover, price and a Buy now button. The books are picked from the topics you chose, so changing your interests in your profile changes what you see.

Search now finds books

Search a title or a topic like “money” or “faith” and the matching books appear with a Books tab, ready to buy.

Buy a book without an account

Share the good news with friends: anyone can buy a book with just a name and an email, and it arrives by email. If they sign up later with the same email, their books are waiting in their library. Signing up now confirms your email with a quick 6-digit code.

Better full-screen viewing

Reels and photos now fill the whole screen on iPhone, photos open centred, and your feed stays exactly where you left it when you close them.

Link previews in posts

Paste a link into a post and it turns into a clean preview card with the title, image and site, right in the composer and on the post.

How to use it
  1. Start a new post.
  2. Paste any link.
  3. The preview card appears. Publish as usual.

Tap any image to view it

Tap a photo in a post and it opens full screen, with the like, comment and share buttons right there so you never have to go back.

Share posts straight to a chat

Found something a friend should see? Send a post or article to someone directly in Messages.

How to use it
  1. Tap the share icon on a post or article.
  2. Choose a chat.
  3. It lands in their conversation.

Messages

Chat with anyone on MindShift Books. Message writers, creators and other readers directly.

Voice notes, photos and videos
Send them right inside the chat. Pick several photos and they arrive together as one album.
Reactions
Press and hold a message to react with an emoji.
Replies
Reply to a specific message so everyone knows what you’re answering.
How to start
  1. Tap the Messages icon, or
  2. Open any profile and tap the Message button.

Videos, reel-style

Tap any video in your feed and it opens full screen. Keep swiping up and the next video starts playing, so you can watch one after another without going back to the feed.
